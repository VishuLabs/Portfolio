ABOUT

I work at the intersection of data engineering, analytics, and teaching — building the pipeline, then explaining what it means.

Python SQL Power BI Excel PostgreSQL Apache Superset Cloud (AWS/Azure)

I'm a data analyst and data engineer who builds the full path from raw source to decision-ready dashboard — designing star-schema warehouses in PostgreSQL, writing production SQL and Python, and shaping the BI layer in Power BI and Apache Superset so stakeholders can actually act on what they see.

My work has included building large, multi-source datasets — reconciling government and institutional data across ministries and years with careful attention to what's real, what's derived, and what's synthetic — and designing cross-domain correlation frameworks that connect indicators nobody had previously joined.

Alongside delivery work, I teach. I've built structured curricula and reference material for Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI — from beginner-friendly workbooks to deep technical guides — because I think the best data professionals can also explain their reasoning clearly.
